A short daily feed of Wikipedia excerpts

Kyslap is an Android app: a short, finite daily feed of excerpts from Wikipedia and freely licensed images, made for learning. The feed stops when you reach your daily goal.

How it works

  • Word for word. Excerpts are quoted exactly as Wikipedia’s volunteers wrote them. Kyslap does not rewrite, summarize, or make up anything. A gap is marked with “…”.
  • Finite. You choose a daily goal. When you reach it, the feed ends. There is no endless scroll.
  • Credited. Every card carries its source and credit. Tap the credit to see the author, license, and links to the article and its history.
  • Yours. No accounts. Your interests and reading history stay on your phone, apart from the requests the app makes to Wikimedia.

Status

Kyslap is in development and is not yet publicly available. A closed test comes first.
