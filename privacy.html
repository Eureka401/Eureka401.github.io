Privacy policy

Draft, last updated 2026-09-30. Covers the Kyslap closed-test version (Android).

The short version

Kyslap has no accounts and no ads in this version. Your interests, goal, reminder time, and reading history stay on your phone. The app does not send them to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Kyslap stores these in a database on your device: the topics you picked, your daily goal, streak and reminder preferences, which articles you have seen, how you interacted with them (for example skipped or saved), per-topic statistics, your daily progress, and items queued for later review. The app uses them to choose what to show you next. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it.

What is sent over the network

To show a bite, the app requests article excerpts and images from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (services of the Wikimedia Foundation). Like any web request, this reveals your device's IP address and the request itself (for example, which article) to Wikimedia. Each request carries a User-Agent that names the app and this website, as Wikimedia's API policy asks; it contains no personal identifier. Wikimedia handles that data under its own privacy policy. When you follow a link to an article or a license, your phone's browser opens that site directly.

Notifications

You can choose a reminder time, which is saved on your phone with your other settings. This version does not send notifications. If a later version does, this policy will say how before it is released.

Crash reports

Kyslap sends crash reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so we can fix bugs. A report contains the error message, the app's code trace at the moment of the crash, the app version, basic device details such as the Android version and phone model, and a random ID created when the app is installed, so we can tell how many phones hit the same bug. That ID is not linked to your name or account, and it resets if you reinstall the app. Reports do not include your name, email address, topics, reading history, or any other data from the app's database, and we do not send your IP address on purpose and have asked Sentry not to store it (Sentry's servers still see it when the report arrives). Session replay and performance tracing are off. Sentry handles the reports under its own privacy policy. Crash reports are on by default; turn them off in Settings under "Send crash reports". Errors stop being sent at once, and the native crash handler stops after you restart the app. The app does not collect analytics.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, advertising ID, or payment information. We run no servers that receive data from the app.

Children

Kyslap is for people 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

If a later version adds anything that collects or shares data (for example ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
