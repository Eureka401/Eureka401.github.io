3. Your account

You do not need an account to use Kyslap. If you sign in with Google, you can write questions and share answers. Before you write or share, the app asks you to accept these terms, and it stores the version you accepted and when, with your account. What we store about your account is described in the privacy policy.

Your display name starts as a generated name such as “Reader 1234”. You can change it, but a name may not imitate Wikipedia, the Wikimedia Foundation or the Kyslap team, and please do not use your real name. We may reset a name that breaks this rule, and we may suspend an account that is used to harm others.

You can delete your account at any time, in the app (Settings, Account) or on the delete-account page of this website. Deleting it removes what you wrote with it, as the privacy policy explains, and your copies leave our backups within 28 days. We cannot withdraw a license from copies others have already made.

Readers can report a question, an answer or a person from inside the app, and hide or block someone on their own device. If you are worried about the safety of a young person, email kyslap.app@gmail.com.
