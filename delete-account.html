Delete your account

Use this page to delete a Kyslap account. You only have one if you signed in with Google inside the app; the app works without one. You can also delete it in the app: Settings, then Account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and display name, your questions, your answers, your votes and follows, and your reports.
  • Copies in our encrypted backups are removed within 28 days.
  • Your reading history and learning progress are on your phone, not in your account. Deleting your account in the app deletes that account’s history on the phone too. Deleting it here cannot reach your phone, so that history stays there until you clear the app’s storage (Android Settings, Apps, Kyslap, Storage, Clear data) or uninstall the app.
  • We cannot withdraw a license from copies other people already made, and we cannot remove screenshots.

This cannot be undone.

1. Sign in with the Google account you used in the app

This signs you in to Kyslap only to delete the account. If this Google account has no Kyslap account, the sign-in briefly creates an empty one, and this page deletes it at once. Google’s sign-in button loads from Google, so Google sees this visit and may set its own cookies; the page itself sets none and keeps nothing after you close it.

2. Confirm

You are signed in.

Deleting now removes the account described above. If this is the wrong Google account, close this page and start again.

Cannot sign in?

Email kyslap.app@gmail.com from the address of the Google account you used, with the subject “Delete my Kyslap account”, and we will delete it for you.

How we handle your data is in the privacy policy.
